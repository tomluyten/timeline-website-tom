About

[Tom Luyten, Hoeselt, BE]

I am an artist, researcher, designer and musician. What connects it all is technology, used as a material to make things people can feel.

I lead the research group Resilient Design Practice at Zuyd Hogeschool in Maastricht, after a PhD at Maastricht University on interactive art in elderly care. As creative director of RipperDocs I design masks, helmets and stage wear for artists. With Tahnee I make conceptual work as Machine Mother, and I play in Pink Ballet Blackout.

  1. 2026
    The ever changing shape of the design practice to comeNADR, with Karin Slegers, Theo Ploeg and Raoul Boers.research001

    A resilient design practice in the age of generative AI and societal transitions. As design spreads beyond the discipline, through design thinking and now generative AI, making is no longer the designer's exclusive domain, while societal transitions pull designers into new roles.

    The paper describes four roles for the professional designer: systems thinker, connector and convener, designer and maker, leader and storyteller. Their foundation is Bildung: mastery combined with responsibility.

    Contribution to the NADR knowledge cycle 2026, "If everybody designs, what makes a designer?", presented at Dutch Design Week.

  7. Selected workExpo: Our Machine MotherExhibition with Tahnee. Artichoque Art Gallery, Maastricht. May 1 to 4.art007

    Our Machine Mother is the shared practice of Tom and Tahnee. It asks how people and technology attract and repel each other.

    Where my own work uses technology as a brush, Machine Mother makes technology the subject and the counterpart. The work is conceptual, physical and often asks the visitor to take part.

    The first exhibition ran for four days in Maastricht. Four installations, each around one question:

    This Circle is Vicious. A cylinder of old computer screens and LED panels. Live generated visuals and a soundscape by composer Jaimy Langenweg pull the visitor into an endless cycle of building up and decay.

    Value is in the Eye of the Beholder. Three canvases with red paint and a counter. The price rises while you look and drops as soon as attention fades.

    Synthetic Realities. A television from the sixties shows a landing on Mars that never happened.

    Ora et Labora. Kneel on an old prayer stool, type in your profession, and the system shows how AI could take over your work in the years to come.

    Also on show: the Machine dialogues and Moving Machines. Two opening nights with a lecture, on 1 and 2 May.

Art.

I write rule sets that draw. Each machine is a piece of code, each output a unique image. Generative work and data-art since 2013, shown as prints, on screens and in installations. Conceptual work on humans and machines runs under the name Machine Mother.

  1. 2021Vensterr: Rraam and RrailsInteractive windows for care homes. FiftyLab.design001

    Vensterr grew out of my research on interactive art in elderly care.

    The installations bring the outside world into a care home as a slow, moving view: a window in the living room, a train compartment in a corridor. They are built to be left on all day and to invite a conversation, not to demand attention.
